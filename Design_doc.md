This project is to create a web page that tracks commute costs and adds them into salary requirements for a job. 

I have been working remotely long enough that commute costs are not part of my salary calculations. 


# Assumptions
- Current car gets 12/16mpg city/highway
- average fuel price is $5.45 
- current salary is 192k/year
- currently working 45 weeks a year 
    - 10 days are holidays
    - 5 sick
    - rest are vacation
- work week is 5 days
- commutes are both ways (so 25 miles here to there is doubled to get home)
